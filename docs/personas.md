# NeuroScript AI - Learner Personas
> **Milestone 1 Deliverable (WP1.1)** • Course: CS-400 Final Year Project, UET Taxila

---

### Persona 1: Bilal – The ADHD Learner
* **Age:** 21 • Undergraduate Computer Science Student
* **Background:** Often takes voluminous handwritten lecture notes during fast-paced lectures but struggles to sit down and review them later due to executive dysfunction and focus fatigue.
* **Pain Points:**
  * Long study decks (20+ flashcards) trigger immediate avoidance and procrastination.
  * Cluttered, busy interfaces with moving elements shatter concentration.
  * Difficulty tracking study streaks without visible, gamified feedback loops.
* **Goals & Success Metrics:**
  * Micro-sessions capped strictly at **5 cards** per review round.
  * Clear visual progress rings showing immediate milestone completion.
  * High-contrast, clean minimalist layout with minimal cognitive distraction.

---

### Persona 2: Sara – The Dyslexic Learner
* **Age:** 20 • Undergraduate Software Engineering Student
* **Background:** Struggles with reading fatigue, letter inversion, and visual glare when reading high-contrast black text on pure white digital displays.
* **Pain Points:**
  * Harsh stark contrast causes visual stress, headaches, and eye fatigue.
  * Crowded letter and line spacing leads to skipping lines or re-reading sentences.
  * Difficulty quickly verifying whether an AI-generated summary actually matched what she wrote.
* **Goals & Success Metrics:**
  * Warm, anti-glare off-white/cream background (`#FBF8EE`) with softened dark charcoal text.
  * Relaxed typography with generous line-height ($\ge 1.5$) and letter spacing.
  * Line-level source provenance ("Where is this from?") to immediately verify the source sentence.
  * Text-to-Speech (TTS) read-aloud support.

---

### Persona 3: Hamza – The Autistic Learner
* **Age:** 22 • Undergraduate Data Science Student
* **Background:** Highly structured, detail-oriented learner who experiences sensory overload from hyper-saturated colors or abrupt interface animations.
* **Pain Points:**
  * Overly saturated colors, loud banners, and unsolicited UI animations cause sensory overload.
  * Ambiguous, figurative, or metaphorical phrasing in study notes creates cognitive ambiguity.
  * Unpredictable navigation layouts or hidden destructive buttons cause high anxiety.
* **Goals & Success Metrics:**
  * Low-sensory pastel color palette (muted sage and soft slate `#3D6B7A`).
  * Explicit step counters ("Card 2 of 5") and predictable, consistent layouts.
  * Respect for system `reduce-motion` settings.
  * "Simplify wording" toggle that removes idioms and converts notes into literal language.
